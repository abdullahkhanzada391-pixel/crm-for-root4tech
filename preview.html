info Quick Guide: Adding and Removing Leads
1 How to Add a New Lead

Click the blue "+ Add New Lead" button. Fill in the modal form (Full Name, Company, Phone, Email, Country, Service, Notes, and Status), then click "Save Lead". It immediately appears at the top of your list.

2 How to Remove Previous Leads
  • Single Delete: Click the red Delete button on any lead's row.
  • Bulk Delete: Check individual row checkboxes or the header checkbox, then click "Delete Selected" in the action bar.
  • Clear All: Click the "Clear All Leads" button to wipe all previous lead records.
